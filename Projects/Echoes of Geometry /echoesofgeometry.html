Echoes Of Geometry

Style:

Area:

A Harmonious Blend of Tradition, Modernity & Thoughtful Design

A home that balances rooted tradition with modern elegance. Designed in close collaboration with a thoughtful client couple, this space reflects their cultural depth, global perspective, and eye for detail. Every corner was shaped through meaningful dialogue—resulting in a home that is calm, intentional, and deeply personal.

Foyer

The foyer makes an immediate impression—inviting yet understated, premium yet grounded. The floating acrylic ceiling with cascading chandeliers, the door, and elegantly detailed wall panels come together to create an entryway that is both captivating and serene. Functional elements like the concealed key storage in entry shelf with brown glass panelling subtly elevate the space, making functionality a part of the visual story.

Living room

The living room reflects a free-flowing narrative of textures, forms, and materials. The stone-finished TV backdrop, fluted wall treatments, and soft, textured wallpapers provide layered depth and tactile richness. Strategically placed glass partitions ensure seamless spatial flow, while track light adds a contemporary, gallery-like ambience. The false ceiling gently transitions to highlight the dining space, creating zones without barriers.

Kitchen

The kitchen was designed as a clean, calm-inspired space—a harmonious blend of efficiency and aesthetics, customized around the clients' cooking styles and utility needs.

Gallery

The passageway—often overlooked in many homes—was thoughtfully transformed with inviting panelling, creating a sense of elongation and expansiveness. Behind these panels lies hidden storage, seamlessly integrated without compromising design integrity.

Pooja area

The Pooja area emerges as an artistic sanctuary—vibrant tiles,sandwiched glass profile pocket design shutter , a brass idol, and a diamond-cut crystal pendant light together create a space that feels both spiritual and visually arresting.

Son's Bedroom

The son’s bedroom brings a youthful sophistication with its muted greys and warm wooden accents. The use of fluted punning behind the bed introduces depth and texture, while the foldable, mobile study table offers flexibility for evolving needs.

The Master Bedroom

With a cane-laminate bed anchoring the space, this bedroom strikes a balance between warmth and texture. The materiality is soft yet rooted, aligning with the overall design intent. Every detail—from panelling to lighting—echoes the client’s aesthetics and brings a grounded elegance to the room.
