Echoes Of Geometry

Style:

Area:

Project Overview

Echoes of Geometry is a celebration of clean lines, composed warmth, and lived-in elegance. The home was designed for a family that values both visual harmony and emotional comfort — rooted in tradition, yet drawn to modern minimalism. Across each space, geometric detailing, functional layouts, and curated textures come together to create a cohesive narrative of calm and clarity.

Foyer & TV Unit

The foyer and TV wall serve as the home's first impression — understated yet confident. A textured stone-finish backdrop frames the floating TV unit, layered with ambient lighting and warm wood accents. Adjacent open shelves, styled with personal mementoes and greenery, create an inviting sense of rhythm. Just steps away, a gallery wall of family photos and the word “laugh” remind us: this space is lived in, not just designed.

Living room

The living room is grounded in serenity. Cream-toned seating, soft lighting, and a muted gallery wall define the space. The textures are subtle, the lines are clean, and the warmth comes from what’s personal — the photographs, the soft furnishings, and the curated stillness. A chevron-patterned wooden door adds both contrast and craftsmanship, anchoring the room with natural detail.

Kitchen & Dining

Rooted in simplicity, the dining area features a classic 4-seater with a corner bench and fluted base, set beneath a trio of pendant lights. A statement wall clock brings both nostalgia and character. This is a space meant for slow meals and steady conversations — functional, timeless, and thoughtfully proportioned. Wrapped in soft teal tones, this kitchen brings a sense of quiet energy to the heart of the home. Clean lines, concealed storage, and subtle textural play come together to create a space that’s both highly functional and aesthetically grounded. Every shelf, every surface is designed to support ease — whether it’s daily cooking or slow weekend meals.

Gallery

The gallery acts as both a transitional and emotional space — where photos speak, textures echo, and light softens every wall. It’s a visual pause between rooms, reminding us that geometry, like life, finds meaning in moments of reflection.

Lilac Layers

Playful meets polished in Room, where deep mauve tones blend with natural wood finishes. The compact layout is made efficient through smart storage and floating elements.While the open floor plan maintains a feeling of ease and openness.

Master bed room- Muted Sanctuary

Subtle, balanced, and deeply restful Room is a masterclass in tonal layering. A full wall headboard in soft beige is paired with a minimalist bed, warm wooden ceiling accents, and built-in side shelves. Natural light fills the space, amplifying its calm geometry. This is a room that doesn’t ask for attention — it holds it quietly.

Divine Corner

This space brings soul into structure. The mandir, custom-designed with arched niches and miniature brass bells, sits beneath a glowing dome — spiritual, sculptural, and serene. Beside it, a writing desk anchors the room in everyday utility. Together, they reflect a family’s values: rooted in ritual, yet open to the rhythm of modern living.
